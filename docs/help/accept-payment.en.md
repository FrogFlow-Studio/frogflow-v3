# How to accept payment

keywords: payment, pay, requisites, bank details, qr, receipt, kaspi, transfer, money, how to pay, buyer pays

The buyer pays the order using your payment details and sends a receipt. You confirm the payment yourself, or the bot checks the receipt automatically.

1. Open [Payment and settings](/admin/settings), the "Payment details and instructions" section.
2. Add a payment method: the details for the buyer's country and, if you have one, a QR code. The first country in the list is the home one: prices are calculated by it until the buyer's country is known.
3. Turn the method on ("Active"). The buyer sees the details when placing the order.
4. When a receipt arrives you get a "New order and receipt" notification: check the amount and confirm the payment in the [order](/admin/orders) card.

If automatic receipt reading is connected (a paid option, the rate is shown in the settings card), a matching receipt needs no action from you, and a doubtful one comes to you with the reason. Card methods (for example Robokassa) are set up in the same settings and switched on separately.
