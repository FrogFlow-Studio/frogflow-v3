# AI balance

keywords: ai balance, ai, neural network, top up, balance ran out, consultant not answering, receipt recognition, smart search, spending, dollars
group: guide
order: 490
pages: /admin/ai-balance

The AI consultant, the product text assistant, smart search and receipt recognition spend this balance at the price of AI requests. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → AI balance". The page is in the owner's menu.

## Remaining {#left}

The balance amount in dollars. The "almost empty" label means less than $1 is left. "empty — AI stopped": AI stops working: new consultant dialogs go to a manager, you check receipts yourself, and smart search does not match products.

A new shop is credited a starting $5 on its first AI request.

## Top up {#topup}

Only the owner can top up.

1. In "Amount, $", enter an amount from 5 to 500 dollars. Under the field is the approximate amount in tenge at the current rate.
2. Press "Issue invoice".
3. Pay with the "Pay in Kaspi" button or by bank transfer using the payment details.
4. Send the receipt to your bot. The balance is topped up as soon as the receipt passes the check.

## Journal {#journal}

All balance movements: "Starting balance", "Top-up", "Operator adjustment" and spending by day: how many requests and how much went to "AI consultant", "AI assistant (product texts)", "Smart search" and "Receipt recognition".
