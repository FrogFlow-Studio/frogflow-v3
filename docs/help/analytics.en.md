# Sales analytics

keywords: analytics, statistics, revenue, average order, funnel, sources, tags, utm, top products, cohorts, repeat purchases, profit, report, charts, analitika

group: guide

order: 300

pages: /admin/analytics

Revenue, orders, buyers and the funnel for a period. Paid orders are counted: delivered digital ones and physical ones with an accepted deposit, minus refunds. The comparison is with the same period before. Where to find it: "Menu → Analytics → Analytics". The section exists if analytics is connected; the accountant sees it too.

## Period and export {#period}

The "7 days", "30 days", "90 days" buttons. "Download CSV" — revenue, orders and average order by day, channel and currency. "Products (CSV)" — units, orders and revenue by product.

## Main numbers {#tiles}

"Revenue", "Paid orders", "Average order", "New customers", "Paying customers". Tiles and charts are in the shop's main currency: orders in roubles, som and other currencies are converted at today's rate. Below the tiles is revenue in the original currencies and what it comes to in the main one.

## Buyer funnel {#funnel}

How many buyers reached each step: "Arrived", "Cart", "Order", "Paid" — and the percentage of the previous step; for the storefront also "Visits" and "Viewed a product". You can choose a channel. Visits and views are counted only on the storefront. Orders entered manually are not in the funnel.

## Funnel by source {#sources}

The source is the buyer's first link: a tag in the link to the bot or a tag in the link to the storefront. This shows which post or ad brings sales.

## Tagged links {#links}

In the "Tagged links" card, make up a "Tag" for the place where you will put the link (a blogger, an ad, a business card), and copy the ready link to the bot or storefront. Orders from it appear as a separate line in the funnel by source.

## Charts {#charts}

"Revenue by day", "Paid orders by day", "New customers by day", "Top products by units sold", "Orders by channel", "All orders in the period by status".

## Customers and repeat purchases {#customers}

"Cohorts and repeat purchases" — what share of a month's buyers bought again. "Customers" and "Returning customers" — new and returning ones, the best buyers and those who have not bought for a long time (the button opens a broadcast to them).

## Order speed {#speed}

How many minutes pass from placing to payment and from payment to delivery. A long wait for delivery is a reason to confirm receipts faster.

## Why sales changed {#explain}

"Explain with AI" compares the last days with the previous ones and writes 3–5 points. Each analysis spends the shop's AI balance.

## What to promote, sales and bundles {#promote}

"What to promote" — tips based on past sales and approved reviews (this is arithmetic, not a forecast). "Sale results" — sales of the promoted products during the sale against the same period before. "Bundles and gifts" — bundle sales and buyers' savings.

## Profit {#profit}

If profit tracking is connected: gross profit is revenue minus [cost](/admin/cost). Products without a cost are not included in profit.
