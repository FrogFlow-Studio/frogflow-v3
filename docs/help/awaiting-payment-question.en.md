# The buyer asks a question while the order awaits payment

keywords: question after order, bot silent after order, awaiting payment, scammers, where will the materials come, want more, how to pay, not answering after order, oplata
group: trouble
order: 40

While an order awaits payment, the bot does not answer the buyer's questions by itself, so as not to say too much. It reacts only to a receipt and to words like "paid", and leaves everything else to you.

1. Open [Inbox](/admin/chat): in Instagram such dialogs stay unread, and if the buyer writes to the Telegram bot, you get a notification.
2. Answer the buyer yourself: from "Inbox" or right from the Instagram app.
3. If the buyer wants to add another product, it is easier to reject the current order in [Orders](/admin/orders) and ask them to place a new one with all the products, or to confirm this one and place a second one.

## Common questions and what to answer {#answers}

- "Where will the materials come?" — to this same conversation right after the payment is confirmed; if "Email after payment" is on, also by email.
- "Are you sure this is not a scam?" — give a link to your profile and reviews, explain that the materials come right after the receipt is checked.
- "Can I pay on receipt?" — digital materials are paid before delivery; explain that checking the receipt takes a few minutes.
- "How do I pay from another country?" — see [A buyer from Russia](/admin/help?a=buyer-russia) and [Payment via Kaspi](/admin/help?a=kaspi).

If you answered the buyer yourself, the bot stays silent in this dialog for the time set in "Bot pause after you reply by hand" in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#dbot-instagram). At the payment step the bot accepts a receipt even during the pause.
