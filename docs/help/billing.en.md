# Subscription payment history

keywords: payment history, invoices, payments, subscription, plan payment, bill, invoice, subscription receipt, accountant
group: guide
order: 480
pages: /admin/billing

Payments and invoices for your FrogFlow subscription, not customer orders. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → Payment history" or the "Payment history" button on the [Modules](/admin/modules) page. Seen by the owner and the accountant.

## Payments {#payments}

Confirmed subscription payments: the period, amount and payment date. Newest first.

## Invoices {#invoices}

All issued invoices: date, amount and status.

- "Issued, awaiting payment": the invoice is created, no payment yet.
- "Receipt under review": you sent a receipt, the operator is checking it.
- "Paid": the confirmation date is shown next to it.
- "Rejected": the reason is written below; pay again or write to [Support](/admin/support).
- "Cancelled": the invoice is no longer valid.

Long lists are paged with the "Back" and "Next" buttons. A new invoice is issued on the [Modules](/admin/modules) page with the "Pay" button.
