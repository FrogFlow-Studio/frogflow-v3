# Bot preview

keywords: preview, how the bot looks, check the bot, see the bot, product card in the bot, bot buttons, bot menu, test
group: guide
order: 500
pages: /admin/bot-preview

The page shows how your bot looks to a buyer in Telegram with the current settings and modules. Nothing is sent to anyone, and no orders are created. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → Bot preview". Available to everyone except the accountant.

## What is shown {#feed}

The feed follows the buyer's steps:

- "The customer picks a language": language choice, if the shop works in several languages.
- "The customer taps "Start"": the greeting. Its text is changed on the [Bot texts](/admin/bot-texts) page.
- "The customer opens a product": the product card with buttons. The price is shown for the default country, with any active offer.
- "Buttons at the bottom of the screen": the bot menu.

If the catalog has no active products, there is no product card: add a product first.

## Customer language {#language}

If the shop has several languages, choose in "Customer language" which language to view the bot in. The feed is redrawn in that language.

## Product for the card {#product}

By default one of your products is shown. To see another one:

1. Type part of the name in "Find a product by name".
2. Press the search button.
3. Choose the product in the list.

## If you have an AI consultant without sales {#consultant}

The consultant answers in its own way, so instead of the feed there is an "Open the consultant test chat" button: there you can ask a question and see the answer.
