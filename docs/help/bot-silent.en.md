# Why the bot does not answer

keywords: bot is silent, not answering, not working, no reply, messages not coming, webhook, token, telegram, instagram, whatsapp, autoresponder

Check in this order.

1. Telegram bot. In [Payment and settings](/admin/settings), the "Telegram bot" block, run the check. If the webhook is not set or points to another address, press "Fix". If the token is invalid, paste a new one from BotFather.
2. Instagram and WhatsApp. The "Instagram and WhatsApp" block should say "working". If it says "needs reconnecting", connect the account again (see the article "How to connect Instagram").
3. Autoresponder mode. With "Orders only" the bot answers "start", "catalog", your trigger word, a material code, buttons and a receipt, and stays silent on ordinary messages: the dialog stays unread and you see it in [Inbox](/admin/chat). To make the bot answer everything, choose "All messages".

If nothing helps, write to [Support](/admin/support): name the channel and the time when the bot did not answer.
