# Catalog categories

group: guide

order: 110

pages: /admin/categories

Categories are the catalog tree in the bot, the Mini App and the storefront. Products without a category are shown on the first screen. Where to find it: "Menu → Catalog → Categories". A manager and above can change them.

## Category list {#list}

The "Name", "Order", "Visible" columns. "Edit" opens the category in the form, "Delete" deletes it after confirmation. "+ Category" creates a new one.

## New category {#form}

1. Enter the "Name".
2. Choose the "Parent category" — the category becomes a nested folder. "— catalog root —" is the top level.
3. Set the "Order": the smaller the number, the higher the category in the list.
4. Press "Save".

## Show to customers {#visible}

With the tick on, the category is visible in the catalog. Off — buyers do not see the category in the catalog; the products themselves and their settings do not change.

## Top row on the storefront {#top-row}

Which categories the buyer sees first in the Mini App and on the storefront. "Add a category…" adds one to the row, "Up" and "Down" change the order, "Remove" takes it out of the row, "Save" stores it. An empty list — the root categories are shown.
