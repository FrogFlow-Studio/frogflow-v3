# Product collections

keywords: collections, collection, selection, link to products, gifts, new arrivals, landing page, storefront, product picks, podborki

group: guide

order: 120

pages: /admin/collections

A collection is a set of products behind one link: "Gifts under 10,000 ₸", "October new arrivals". You can send the link to the bot, Instagram or a broadcast. Where to find it: "Menu → Catalog → Collections". The section exists if the storefront is connected.

## New collection {#form}

1. Press "New collection".
2. Enter the "Name".
3. In "Products in the collection", find products by search and add them; "Remove" excludes a product.
4. Press "Save collection".

The "not shown to buyers right now" mark means the product is hidden or sold out: in a collection buyers see only products that are on sale now. "product no longer exists" means the product was deleted.

## Collection link {#link}

"Copy link" copies the address of the collection on the storefront. "Edit collection" opens it in the form.

## Delete {#delete}

"Delete" deletes the collection after confirmation. Its link will stop opening; the products do not change.
