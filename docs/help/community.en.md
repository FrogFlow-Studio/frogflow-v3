# Authors community

A place where FrogFlow shop authors ask questions, share experience, look for contractors, message each other and run joint promotions. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → Community". Available to everyone except the accountant; the owner, administrators and managers can write, others can only read.

## How to join the community {#join}

You can read and write only when "Show me in the community" is on in the "Authors community" card on the [Payment and settings](/admin/settings) page. The "Settings → Community" link is at the bottom of the page. Details are in the article [Settings: shop](/admin/help?a=settings-shop).

Other authors see the community, so do not publish order amounts, customer names or contacts.

## Sections and search {#sections}

- "Questions": ask other authors.
- "Experience": tell what worked.
- "Looking for / offering": with the topics "Packaging", "Photo", "Delivery", "Contractors"; "All" shows everything.

To find a topic, type words in "Search topics" and press "Search". "Show more" loads older topics.

## New topic and replies {#post}

1. Press "New topic".
2. Enter the "Title" (3 to 140 characters) and the "Text" (up to 4000 characters).
3. Press "Publish".

To reply, open the topic, type text in "Your reply" and press "Reply". "← Back to list" returns to the topics. There is a limit on the number of topics and replies per hour and per day to prevent spam.

## Report {#report}

"Report" on a topic or reply sends a complaint. When several complaints add up, the post is hidden automatically.

## Direct messages {#dm}

The "Direct messages" tab is one-on-one messaging with other authors; the number next to the tab is unread messages.

- "Write" next to an author in the "Community authors" list opens a conversation; type a "Message" and press "Send".
- "Do not let this author write to me": they can no longer write to you. Such authors are shown under "Not allowed to write to you"; "Remove the block" lets them write again.

## Do not accept direct messages {#dm-off}

The "Do not accept direct messages" checkbox: authors cannot write to you. Earlier conversations stay.

## Joint promotions {#joint}

The tab is available with the "Discounts and promo codes" module. Two or three shops run a promotion together: each has its own promo code and its own discount at its own expense.

1. Press "Propose a promotion".
2. Enter the "Promotion name" and choose "Valid until".
3. In "With whom (one or two authors)", tick the partners.
4. In "Your terms", set "Your promo code", "Your discount, %" and, optionally, "Conditions".
5. Press "Send the proposal".

The partner sees the invitation, enters their promo code and discount and presses "Accept" or "Decline". After agreeing, your terms can no longer be changed here. Each participant's promo code appears in "Promotions and discounts" and turns on when everyone agrees; a "Partner promotion" appears on each participant's storefront. The promotion runs until the chosen date. The platform does not collect or split money: orders stay in each shop.

"Decline" or "Cancel the promotion" by any participant cancels the whole promotion for everyone, and the participants' promo codes are turned off.
