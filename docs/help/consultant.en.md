# AI consultant

group: guide
order: 270

The AI consultant answers buyers' free-form questions in Telegram, Instagram Direct and WhatsApp, based on your profile, knowledge base and catalog. It hands complex cases to you in "Chat" and sends a request to Telegram. Where to find it: "Menu → Marketing → AI consultant". If you have a consultant workspace (a price-list consultant without sales), this page is called "Bot settings" and sits in the "Consultant" group.

Only the shop admin or owner can save the consultant settings. If the page shows a yellow warning that the consultant isn't set up on the platform, it won't answer yet, but you can fill in the fields in advance.

## Enable the consultant {#enable}

The "Enable consultant" switch.

On — the consultant replies to the buyer's free-form text in the bot and in conversations.

Off — for free-form text the bot shows the menu, and the consultant stays silent.

## When a person replies, the bot stays silent {#pause}

How long the bot stays silent after a person has replied: 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or "Until I hand it back". The pause starts when the consultant hands the conversation to a manager, when you reply from "Chat" and when you reply directly in Instagram or WhatsApp. This way the bot doesn't interrupt you mid-conversation.

## Business profile {#profile}

What the consultant knows about you. Filled in if the consultant doesn't answer from a price list (otherwise there will be an "Open «Consultant price list»" button here, and all of this is set there).

- "Company name" — required.
- "Website" — optional.
- "Price currency" and "Countries" (comma-separated: KZ, RU).
- "About the company" — what you sell, to whom, what sets you apart, 2–3 sentences.
- "Delivery terms" and "Payment terms" — the consultant will retell them to the buyer.

## Conversation goal and tone {#goal}

"Conversation goal" — where the consultant steers the conversation:

- "Sales: lead to an order";
- "Qualification and a call";
- "Booking a service or a visit";
- "Answers from the knowledge base".

"Tone of voice": "Friendly expert", "Formal and reserved", "Premium and warm" or "Casual, first-name basis".

"Primary language" — Russian, Kazakh or English; the consultant still answers each buyer in their own language.

## Special instructions {#instructions}

What to say and what not to promise, for example: "don't quote delivery faster than 2 days", "offer a card with a bouquet". The consultant follows these instructions in every reply.

## Supplements disclaimer {#disclaimer}

The "Add a disclaimer about supplements and consulting a doctor" checkbox. On — when relevant, the consultant explains that the product is a dietary supplement, not a medicine, gently advises seeing a doctor and doesn't make diagnoses. Turn it on if you sell supplements. Off — no such explanations.

## Knowledge base {#knowledge}

Documents with terms, answers to frequent questions, service descriptions. The consultant answers only from these texts and the catalog — what isn't here, it doesn't promise.

"+ Document" adds a document: "Document title", "Section", "Document text". "Delete" removes it. Click "Save" at the bottom.

If the consultant answers from a price list, the knowledge base is on a separate page, [Knowledge base](/admin/help?a=knowledge), or in the "Consultant price list".

## Test the replies {#test}

Ask a question as a buyer would, for example "do you deliver to Astana?", and click "Ask". The answer is based on the saved settings — save your changes first. The "handoff to manager" mark means that in a real conversation the consultant would call you in. Nothing is sent to buyers during a test.

In the consultant workspace the test remembers the conversation history; "Start over" clears it.

## Stories {#stories}

Mark which product is shown in a story, reel or post: when a buyer replies to it with "how much is it?", the consultant will understand what they mean.

1. Paste a link to the story, reel or post, or click "Pick from posts".
2. Find the product in the catalog (or in the consultant price list) — or type the name and price manually.
3. Optionally add a "Note for the consultant".
4. Click "Add tag". Up to 10 products can be linked to one post.

"Edit" and "Remove" are on each tag in the list. The block is available when the Instagram module is on.

## Replies to Instagram comments {#comment-reply}

Available only in the consultant workspace and with Instagram connected. Only the shop admin or owner can change the "Reply to comments with the price" switch.

On — the bot replies once in Direct to a comment under a post: it finds the product from the comment and the post caption and quotes the price from the price list. If it can't find the product or price with confidence, it writes "Hello! I'll help with the price, just write here what you're interested in." It never posts anything publicly under the comment. If your [Comment-to-DM rule](/admin/help?a=instagram) fires on the comment, the rule replies: Instagram allows only one private message per comment.

Off — the consultant doesn't reply to comments.

## Email after payment {#email}

Whether to ask an Instagram buyer for their email after the payment is set up not here but in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#email-ask), in the "Email after payment (Instagram)" block.

## Usage {#usage}

How many replies the consultant gave, what it cost and the last error, if any. In the consultant workspace — usage for 7 days.
