# Product cost

keywords: cost, unit cost, cost price, purchase price, expenses, profit, margin, markup, gross profit, accountant, sebestoimost

group: guide

order: 320

pages: /admin/cost

How much each product costs you. Gross profit in [Analytics](/admin/analytics) and reports is calculated from this. Where to find it: "Menu → Analytics → Cost". The section exists if profit tracking is connected. A manager and above can edit it, and so can the accountant; a "view only" employee cannot.

## Product cost {#cost}

The "Cost" field in the product's currency and "Save". Empty means "not set", not zero: such a product is not included in profit. Enter zero only if the product truly has no costs.

## By variant {#variants}

For a product with variants you can set the cost of each variant. A variant without its own amount takes the product's cost ("same as product").

## Only without cost {#only-missing}

The tick leaves products whose cost is not set. At the top — "Without cost: … of …". If products have a cost recorded in a different currency than the price, the panel asks you to enter it again.

The cost also changes when goods in are posted in the [warehouse](/admin/warehouse), if recalculation is switched on there.
