# Customers and the customer card

keywords: customers, buyers, clients, base, customer base, segments, tags, note, points, add points, subtract points, block, ban, birthday, link profiles, contacts export, pokupateli, klienty

group: guide

order: 40

pages: /admin/customers

Everyone who wrote to your bot, in Instagram or WhatsApp, and who bought on the storefront. Here are segments, search and blocking, and in the card — purchase history, tags, notes and points. Where to find it: "Menu → Sales → Customers".

The section is not available to the accountant. A manager and above can change things.

## Customer list {#list}

Columns: "Customer", "Channel", "Contacts", "Points", "Since". Without a search the 200 newest are shown; find the rest with the "Name, @username, phone" search. The name opens the customer card.

## Export to Excel {#export}

The button is there if the export module is connected, and is visible to a manager and above: the file contains buyer contacts.

## Customer segments {#segments}

A segment answers the question "who is in it". You launch a broadcast or a promotion yourself — a segment sends nothing.

- Ready-made: "Bought 2+ times", "Not bought for 60 days", "VIP (tag “VIP”)".
- Choose a segment — you see "In the segment: …" and a table with the number of orders and the last purchase. The "unsubscribed" mark means the person opted out of broadcasts.
- "Show all customers" returns the usual list.

## Your own segment {#segment-custom}

1. Enter the "Segment name".
2. Choose a rule: "At least this many orders", "Days without a purchase" or "Seller tag".
3. Set the "Value" and press "Save segment".

"Delete" removes a saved segment. The customers do not change.

## Block {#block}

The button is there if the blocking module is connected, and works only for buyers from Telegram. A blocked person is marked "blocked", and the bot stops answering them. "Unblock" returns things as they were.

## Customer card {#card}

Opens from the name in the list or from "Inbox". At the top — "Open conversation" (if "Inbox" is connected) and the "blocked" and "unsubscribed from broadcasts" marks.

- "Profile": channel, phone, email, language, country, points, since what date they are with you, the consultant's last reply, where they came from.
- "Purchases": how many orders are paid, the purchase total, the first and last purchase.
- "Referrals": who invited this buyer and whom they invited.
- "Reviews", "Orders", "Requests", "Pipeline leads", "Recent messages" — with links.

## Birthday {#birthday}

The "Birthday" field in the profile, for example 15.03. Press "Save". If birthday greetings are switched on in [broadcasts](/admin/broadcasts), the bot greets the buyer on that day, once a year. There is no need to enter the year. The buyer can also set the date themselves — through a link from the bot.

## Tags and note {#tags}

- "Tags" — separated by commas. You can pick a broadcast audience by tags and build a "Seller tag" segment.
- "Note for the team" — visible only to employees.

Press "Save". If a colleague has just changed the note, the panel shows their version and asks you to check and save again — so someone else's edit is not lost.

## Adjust points {#points}

Available if the loyalty program is connected, for a manager and above.

1. Choose "Add" or "Subtract".
2. Enter "How many points".
3. Write the "Reason (shown in the activity log)" — at least 3 characters.
4. Press "Apply".

You will see "Balance: before → after". Points do not go below zero. The adjustment is added to the current balance, so points credited by the bot in the same minute are not lost.

## In other channels {#linked}

The same person can write both in Telegram and in Instagram. Profiles with the same phone or email are linked automatically, and their orders and conversations are shown together. To link manually:

1. In the "Link a profile: name, @username or phone" field, find the second profile.
2. Press "Link".

"Unlink" separates the profiles again.
