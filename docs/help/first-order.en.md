# The first order and receipt confirmation

keywords: test order, check, order for myself, confirm, chek, deliver, delivering materials, payment check, confirmation
group: start
order: 5

Before waiting for buyers, walk their path yourself: you will see what a person receives and understand where to confirm the payment.

1. Write a comment with the keyword under the post that has the rule, from your second account (or ask a friend).
2. Reply "+" to the message in Direct and press "Buy".
3. Choose the country and the payment method: the bot sends the amount and the payment details.
4. Send any screenshot to the conversation instead of a receipt.
5. Open [Orders](/admin/orders), the "Receipt review" tab, and open the order.
6. Look at the "Payment receipt" and press "✅ Confirm and deliver".
7. Check in the conversation that the materials arrived.

## How to confirm real receipts {#confirm}

Compare the amount, the recipient and the date on the receipt with what came to your account. If everything matches — "✅ Confirm and deliver": the order becomes "delivered" and the buyer gets the files in the same conversation. If it is the wrong receipt or there is no money — choose "Reject" in the "More" menu, and the buyer gets a notification.

Pressing "Confirm" twice does no harm: the materials will not be sent again. If the delivery failed (for example, the product has no file), the order returns to "Receipt review" with a note in "History": fix the product and press the button again, only the undelivered materials will be sent.

## Receipt notifications {#notify}

A new order and receipt come to you in Telegram with "confirm" and "reject" buttons if your Telegram ID is set in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#notify). Without it, receipts are visible only in "Orders".

There is also a one-click check without money: the "Trial order" button on the [Overview](/admin) checks the price, payment, file and delivery on your product and creates nothing.
