# The first rule with a "Buy" button

keywords: rule, comment, comment to dm, auto reply, buy button, reel, reels, post, direct, dm, automation, code word, keyword
group: start
order: 3

A rule replies in Direct to whoever left a comment under your post or reel. With a "Buy" button the buyer places the order right in the conversation, without a catalog or a cart.

1. Open [Instagram](/admin/instagram) and press "+ New rule".
2. "Name" — anything, for yourself, for example "Palette reel".
3. "Post" — choose a post or reel from the list. A fresh reel may not be in the list yet: paste its link and press "Find by link".
4. "Keywords" — the words to reply to, separated by commas, for example "want, price, +". If you want to reply to any comment under this post, turn on "Reply to any comment".
5. "Direct message" — the first message, for example "Hello! The palette costs 1700 ₸, here is how to get it:".
6. In "Buttons under the message" press "+ Product", find the product and choose it: a "Buy" button appears.
7. Press "Save" and check that the rule shows "active".

## Why the button comes in a second message {#two-step}

Instagram allows only one private message per comment, and in it does not show buttons to people who do not follow you. So with a button the rule always works in "Two steps": first a text goes out asking the person to reply "+", and the "Buy" button comes as soon as they reply. If you have already chatted with this person, the button comes right away.

The reply is awaited for 24 hours. If the person has not replied within 24 hours, the button will not be sent: write to them yourself from "Inbox".

## The product must match the post {#product}

Put in the rule the product the post is about. If the product is not mentioned in the post caption, a warning "The product … is not mentioned in the post" appears when you save: check that you did not pick the wrong post. A rule with someone else's product creates orders for something the buyer did not want.

## What next {#after}

It is worth putting a "Buy" button under every post that sells. If you remove the button from a rule or switch the rule off, orders from under that post stop coming. More about all rule settings: [Instagram: rules for comments](/admin/help?a=instagram).
