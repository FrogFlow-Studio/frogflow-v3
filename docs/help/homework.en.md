# Homework

keywords: homework, assignments, review, accept work, needs revision, curator, curators, quick replies, students, domashka, dz

group: guide

order: 160

pages: /admin/homework

Students' work on course lessons. You accept the work or return it for revision with a comment — the student gets the answer in their channel and sees the feedback on the course page. Where to find it: "Menu → Learning → Homework", the number next to it is work waiting for review. An assignment is added in a course lesson, in the product card.

## Reviewing work {#review}

1. Open the work: lesson, curator, submission date, previous versions.
2. Write "Your feedback" — what worked, what to fix. "Quick reply:" inserts a ready comment.
3. Press "✓ Accept" or "Needs revision".

The student gets a message. If it did not go out, the panel tells you — the student still sees the feedback on the course page. "Mine" shows only the work of your students.

## Curators {#curators}

If several people review. A student's work goes to one curator in Telegram and the student is assigned to them. The curator presses «Принять» or replies to the message with a swipe right in Telegram. Without curators, the seller gets the work.

- "Name", "Telegram id" — where to send work. The curator must first press /start in the shop's bot, otherwise Telegram will not let the bot write to them, and the work comes to the seller with a note.
- "Admin login" — so the curator sees their list in the panel.
- "Cohorts" — which cohorts the curator leads.
- "Add a curator", "Remove", "Save curators".

Students already assigned stay with their curators. "Curator workload" shows how much work is waiting for each.

## How to assign {#curators-mode}

- "Students in turn" — a new student goes to the next curator.
- "By cohort (the rest in turn)" — a cohort's student goes to that cohort's curator.

## Quick replies {#replies}

Ready comments on work. {name} inserts the student's name. "+ quick reply", "Name", "Comment text", "Save quick replies". In Telegram, reply to the work notification with a swipe: "#" shows the list, "#2" takes reply No. 2.
