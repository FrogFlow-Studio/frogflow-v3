# How to connect Instagram

keywords: instagram, insta, direct, dm, connect account, whatsapp, messenger, authorization, reconnect
group: start
order: 2

The bot answers buyers in Direct, runs the catalog and orders, and passes hard questions to you in "Chat".

1. Open [Payment and settings](/admin/settings) and find the "Instagram and WhatsApp" block.
2. Press "Connect Instagram" (for WhatsApp — "Connect WhatsApp"), sign in and grant the requested permissions.
3. Go back to the settings and press "Refresh": the block should show "Connected accounts: 1" and the status "working".

If it says "needs reconnecting", the authorization has expired: connect the account again. If there are no connect buttons, messenger connection is not set up on the platform yet — write to us in [Support](/admin/support).

Automatic replies to comments under posts are set up separately, on the [Instagram](/admin/instagram) page.
