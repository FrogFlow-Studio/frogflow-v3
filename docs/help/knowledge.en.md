Articles the AI consultant takes its answers from: about brands, materials, care, warranty, delivery. The consultant attaches a fitting article to the buyer's question and doesn't make up anything beyond it. Where to find it: "Menu → Consultant → Knowledge base" (in the consultant workspace). In the regular view the same base is at the bottom of the [Consultant price list](/admin/help?a=pricelist) page.

Managers and above can change articles.

## List and search {#list}

"Find an article" searches by title, tags and text, for example "care", "warranty", "Portugal". The "no tags" mark means the consultant will find such an article with more difficulty — add tags to it.

"New article" opens an empty article. If the open article has unsaved changes, the page will ask you to save or close it first.

## Article {#article}

- "Title" — briefly, what the article is about.
- "Tags — the consultant finds the article by them" — comma-separated words buyers use to ask about this: "warranty, repair, broken".
- "Text" — the answer itself. Write it the way you'd like the consultant to explain it to the buyer.

"Save article" saves only the open article. "Delete…" removes it — the consultant will stop using it.

## Load articles from a file or text {#import}

1. "Choose file" (PDF, Word .docx, .txt or .md) or paste text into the "Or paste text" field.
2. Click "Show articles" — a list of the articles found appears.
3. Tick the ones to add. The "updates the article with the same title" mark means the old article will be replaced; "new" — a new one will be added.
4. Click "Add to the base".

To split a file into several articles, separate them with a ### or --- line: the first line after the separator is the title, a "Tags: …" line sets the tags. A file without separators becomes one article named after the file. Only the ticked articles are saved, existing articles stay. Articles that are too long are cut — the page will warn you.
