# Activity log

keywords: activity log, who changed, change history, audit, staff, team actions, who changed settings, log
group: guide
order: 530
pages: /admin/log

Who on the shop team changed what and when: settings, products, texts, the team. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → Activity log". Seen by the owner and administrators. Nothing changes here, it is view-only.

## What goes into the log {#what}

Each row is one action: "When", "Who" (the staff member's username), "Action" and "Details". Newest first. Entries are kept for six months.

Orders do not go here: who changed an order and when is visible in the history inside the order card.

## One person's actions {#who}

In "Person", choose a staff member's username to see only their actions, or "Whole team". The "Journal" button next to a staff member in the [Team](/admin/help?a=settings-team) section leads here too.
