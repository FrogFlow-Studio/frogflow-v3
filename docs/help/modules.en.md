# Modules and your plan

group: guide
order: 470
pages: /admin/modules

The page shows the shop's plan, how many AI limits were used this month and which modules are on. You also pay for the subscription and request new features here. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → Modules". Seen by the owner only.

Modules do not turn on by themselves: any "Enable" button sends a request to the platform operator, who will contact you and turn the module on.

## Your plan {#plan}

The plan name (Basic, Pro or Business) and the price per month; if paid for a year or 3 months, a note about it; the end date of the trial period and the date the subscription is paid until. Extra channels connected on top of the plan are shown as separate labels. If the terms are under a contract, the card says so, and there are no payment buttons.

## AI this month {#plan-usage}

"Consultant dialogs" and "Receipt checks": how much of the plan limit is used. Above the limit, new dialogs go to a manager and receipts come to you for manual review. The limit resets on the 1st of the month. Below it says what the limits are on the next plan.

## Pay for the subscription {#plan-pay}

1. In "Pay for", choose the period: "a month", "3 months" or "a year (12 months for the price of 10)". When paying for 3 months or a year, setup is free.
2. Press "Pay": an invoice appears.
3. Pay by QR or the "Open in Kaspi" button for exactly the invoice amount (or by bank transfer using the payment details if there is no Kaspi link).
4. Send the receipt to your bot: the subscription is extended after the check.

All invoices and payments are in [Payment history](/admin/billing).

## Switch to another plan or add a channel {#plan-upgrade}

The "Upgrade to Pro · …" buttons (during the trial, "Choose …") show the price per month; hovering shows the setup price. The "Add: WhatsApp on top of the plan", "Instagram on top of the plan", "VIP club on top of the plan", "200 AI dialogs pack on top of the plan" buttons add a feature to your current plan for a monthly fee. Pressing sends a request: the button shows "Request sent", and the operator will contact you.

## Module list {#modules-list}

Modules are grouped: "Basics", "Payments", "Catalog", "Instagram", "WhatsApp", "Retention", "Customer service", "Analytics", "Physical goods". Each has a description and a label:

- "enabled" or "in your plan": already working.
- "included": part of the basic features at no extra cost.
- "in Pro" (or another plan): the module is part of that plan and is not sold separately.
- "soon": still in development.

If the shop is not on a plan, a module that is off has an "Enable for … ₸" button (one-time setup price) or "Enable". Pressing sends a request to the operator. Some modules need others: for example, "Shop in Direct" works together with "Instagram automation".
