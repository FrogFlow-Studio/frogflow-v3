# What's new

keywords: news, what's new, updates, new features, changes, poll, voting, platform
group: guide
order: 550
pages: /admin/news

Platform news: new features and important changes. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → What's new"; the number next to the item is unread news. Available to everyone except the accountant.

## News {#feed}

Fresh news is marked "new". When you open the page, all news is marked as read.

A news item may have a button:

- "Learn more" opens a detailed description;
- "Enable" opens the setup of the new feature. Only the owner sees it.

## Polls {#poll}

Sometimes a news item has a "Poll": this is how we learn what to build next. Press an answer option. You can vote once, and after that your choice is shown. The owner, administrators and managers vote.
