# The buyer did not get the materials

keywords: did not get materials, files did not come, did not arrive, delivery, retry delivery, no file, not delivered, where are the materials, send again, resend
group: trouble
order: 100

First open the order in [Orders](/admin/orders) and look at its status and "History".

1. The status is "Receipt review" or "awaiting payment" — you have not confirmed the payment yet. Check the receipt and press "✅ Confirm and deliver".
2. "History" has "Выдача не удалась" (delivery failed) — some materials were not sent. Check that the product in [Products](/admin/products) has a file uploaded in the right language, and press "✅ Confirm and deliver" again: only the undelivered ones will be sent.
3. The status is "delivered", but the buyer says there is nothing — ask them to check the conversation above and the "Requests" folder in Instagram. If email after payment is on, the materials are also in the email.
4. Look at "Undelivered" in the menu (the item appears when there are undelivered messages): you can press "Retry" there.

## The buyer has not answered about the language {#language}

If the product has files in several languages, the bot asks the buyer which one to send. Until they answer, the order stays "delivering" and the files are not sent. Write to the buyer in "Inbox" so they choose a language.

## Instagram blocked the delivery {#window}

If the buyer has been silent for more than 24 hours, Instagram may block the message with the materials. Ask them to write anything in Direct and press "Confirm and deliver" again, or send the link to the materials yourself.
