# Instagram blocks a message to an old conversation

keywords: old conversation, not delivered, instagram error, 24 hour window, 24 hours, did not arrive, code 10, instagram refused, wrote long ago, messaging window
group: trouble
order: 20

Instagram lets a shop write to a person only within 24 hours after their last message. If the person once chatted with you and has been silent for more than 24 hours since, Instagram may block both the button and plain text. There is no way around it: neither the bot nor the platform can write to such a person first.

1. Find these people: on the [Instagram](/admin/instagram) page, in "Trigger history" with "only failed" selected.
2. Reply to them publicly under their comment, for example "Write "Palette" to me in Direct and I will send you the order link".
3. As soon as the person writes in Direct, the window opens for 24 hours: the bot sends the "Buy" button, or you reply yourself from "Inbox".

## How to make this happen less often {#prevent}

In the rule, fill in "If the DM could not be delivered at all": the system itself leaves a public request to write first for those who could not be reached in any way. This works for rules on a specific post and is checked every 15 minutes.

Payment and cart reminders and broadcasts in Instagram also go only to those who wrote to the shop in the last 24 hours. This is an Instagram rule, not a shop setting.
