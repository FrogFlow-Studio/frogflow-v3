# Overview: the panel's home page

keywords: overview, home, dashboard, summary, today, revenue, tasks, checklist, launch, first steps, create, plus create, test order, trial order, limits, obzor, glavnaya

group: guide

order: 10

pages: /admin

"Overview" is the first page after you sign in. It shows what is waiting for your reply, how sales are going today and what is not set up yet. Where to find it: "Menu → Overview", it is always first in the menu. The page refreshes by itself when a new order comes in.

## Waiting for you {#waiting}

Tiles with what needs your action right now:

- "Receipts awaiting review" — orders where the buyer sent a receipt and you have not confirmed the payment yet. Tap to open them as a list in [Orders](/admin/orders).
- "Undelivered" — messages that did not reach buyers even after retries.
- "Inbox" — unread dialogs with buyers.
- "Requests" — open buyer questions.
- "Homework" — students' work waiting for review (for an online school).

A tile is shown only if the matching section is connected. When everything is handled, the card says "All clear: nothing is waiting for your reply."

## Sales today and Growth {#pace}

"Sales today" compares the total of today's paid orders with what there was yesterday by the same hour, and shows sales by hour. "Growth" compares the last 30 days with the previous 30: revenue, paid orders and new buyers. Details are in [Analytics](/admin/analytics).

## Numbers: revenue, products, customers {#stats}

- "Revenue, last 24 hours", "Revenue, last 7 days", "Revenue, 30 days" — the total of paid orders: delivered digital ones and physical ones with an accepted deposit. Refunds are subtracted. It is counted in the shop's main currency; other currencies are not added up.
- "Products on sale" — how many products are switched on now.
- "Customers" — how many people are in your base.

An accountant does not see revenue for the last 24 hours and the last 7 days.

## To do today {#today}

The to-do list for the day. The system adds items here itself: new orders to take into work, orders ready to hand over, products that are running out. Tap a line and the right list opens. The "Refresh" button reloads the data. If a number could not be counted, it says "could not be determined" instead.

## My tasks {#todos}

Below the system items you can keep your own tasks:

1. Type the text into the "Add a task…" field.
2. Pick the day in the "For which day" field.
3. Press "Add".

The tick marks a task as done (you can see who marked it), "Move back to open" removes the mark. An unfinished task moves to the next day with the note "carried over from …". Tasks for future days are in "Later", done ones are in "Done" and are kept for 30 days. A manager and above can add and tick tasks.

## Recent orders {#recent}

A few fresh orders with number, buyer, amount and status. "all orders" opens [Orders](/admin/orders).

## Shop launch {#launch}

A checklist of steps after which the shop sells on its own: connect the bot, add products, set how you get paid, choose pickup and delivery, set where orders and receipts are sent, fill in the seller details, write a greeting and make a test order. A step ticks itself when it is visible in the settings. The nearest undone one is labelled "Next step".

- "Hide" on a step removes it from the list; hidden ones are shown at the bottom in the "Hidden steps" line, with "Restore" there.
- "Hide" on the whole card removes the checklist from the overview completely.

While the "First 7 days" card is open, it is shown instead of this checklist.

## First 7 days {#first-week}

Four steps to the first sale for new shops. A step closes by itself when it is visible in the shop's data: a product is added, payment is set up, the shop link has been sent.

## Test order to yourself {#test-order}

The "Test order to yourself" button opens your bot on a product card. Place an order, send any screenshot instead of a receipt, then confirm the order in [Orders](/admin/orders) — the bot sends you the delivery as it would to a buyer. This way you see the buyer's whole path. If the bot is not connected or there is no enabled product, the card tells you what to do first.

## Trial order {#trial-order}

"Trial order" checks checkout on your product without the bot: the price, payment, file, delivery and notifications. No order, payment or points are created, and nothing gets into reports. The result: "The trial order passed" or a list of what needs fixing.

## Examples for your niche {#template}

The "Start with examples for your niche" block and the "Add examples" button create categories and a few hidden example products. Buyers do not see them. After that [Products](/admin/products) opens: change the photo, price and text and switch them on.

While examples exist, the overview shows a bar with the "Open products" and "Remove examples" buttons. "Remove examples" deletes the created example products and categories. If some were not deleted because of a failure, press it again — it finishes the job.

## Plan limits {#limits}

The card appears when you have used 80% of the monthly limit: "Consultant dialogs" and "Receipt checks". Nothing is switched off: above the limit dialogs go to a manager, and receipts come to you for manual review. The limit resets on the 1st of the month. The button leads to [Modules](/admin/modules), where you can switch to a higher plan.

## You are on the new panel {#moved}

If the shop moved from the old panel, a "You are on the new FrogFlow panel" card is shown at the top: where things are now and what to check before the first orders. "All checked, hide" removes it.

## The "+ Create" menu {#create-menu}

The "+ Create" button sits above the side menu on every page. It holds what you can start right away:

- "New order" — a manual order, only for confectionery and flower shops.
- "New product" — an empty product card.
- "New broadcast" — the broadcasts section.
- "New promotion" — the discounts section; only the shop owner and administrator have this item.
- "Reply in Inbox" — conversations with buyers.

An item appears only if the needed section is connected. An accountant and a "view only" employee do not have the button: they do not create anything.
