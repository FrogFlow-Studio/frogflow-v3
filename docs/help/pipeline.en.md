# Deal pipeline

keywords: pipeline, deals, leads, requests, crm, stages, kanban, board, lost, done, voronka, sdelki

group: guide

order: 60

pages: /admin/pipeline

A board where orders and leads are laid out by deal stage. Where to find it: "Menu → Sales → Pipeline". The section exists if the pipeline module is connected. Any employee except the accountant can view it; a manager and above can add and move leads.

## Stages {#stages}

"New", "In progress", "Awaiting payment", "Paid", "Done", "Lost". Above a column — how many cards and for what amount. Done and lost ones are shown for 30 days.

## Orders on the board {#orders}

An order moves by itself together with its status; it is not moved by hand:

- "awaiting payment" and "receipt review" — "Awaiting payment";
- paid, accepted, in progress, ready, delivering — "Paid";
- delivered — "Done";
- rejected, cancelled, refunded — "Lost".

## New lead {#lead-new}

A lead is a person who has not ordered anything yet. Leads appear by themselves (from the AI consultant, from an Instagram comment, from a conversation) or are added manually:

1. Press "New lead".
2. Fill in "Name", "Phone or handle", "Deal amount" and "Note".
3. Press "Save".

Under a lead you can see where it came from: "added manually", "from the AI consultant", "Instagram comment", "from a conversation".

## Lead stage {#lead-stage}

The "Stage" list on the lead card moves it to another column. You move it yourself.

## Owner and note {#lead-owner}

The "Owner" list — "Unassigned" or a team employee. "Note" — text for the team; "Save" stores it.
