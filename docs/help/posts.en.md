# Posting to Instagram

keywords: post, publish, publication, reel, reels, story, stories, scheduled post, schedule, planner, carousel, autoposting, post to insta
group: guide
order: 240
pages: /admin/posts

Here you publish a post, Reel or story to the shop's Instagram right away or on a schedule, without opening your phone. Where to find it: "Menu → Marketing → Posts". The section is visible when the Instagram module is on; managers and above can publish and cancel posts.

If Instagram isn't connected yet, the page will offer "Connect in settings" (more in [How to connect Instagram](/admin/help?a=instagram-connect)).

## What to publish {#kind}

- "Post" — JPEG or PNG photos, up to 10 files (several files make a carousel). 1080×1350 works best.
- "Reel" — one MP4 or MOV video, vertical 1080×1920.
- "Story" — one photo or a video up to 60 seconds, 1080×1920. Stories show no caption, so there is no "Caption" field for them.

## Accounts {#accounts}

Tick the Instagram accounts to publish to. An account marked "needs reconnecting" can't be selected — reconnect it in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#instagram-whatsapp).

## Post about a product {#assist}

This block appears if you came here from a product card (and it isn't a story). The "✨ Write the text" button — AI writes a caption from the product card and attaches the first photo. Check the text before publishing.

## Caption and files {#text}

"Caption" — up to 2200 characters. If you want to sell through comments, put the product name and a code word in the caption ("Comment «хочу»"), then create a rule for this post on the [Instagram](/admin/help?a=instagram) page.

"Files" — upload photos or videos; "Remove" deletes a file from the post.

## When {#when}

- "Now" — the post goes out right after "Publish".
- "Schedule" — pick a date and time in your device's clock, no more than 30 days ahead. The button changes to "Schedule".

## Recent posts {#recent}

A list of your posts with statuses: "published", "scheduled", "publishing", "failed" (with the reason), "cancelled". "Open" leads to the post on Instagram. "Cancel" cancels a scheduled post before it goes out.

Before sending, the page checks for common mistakes and tells you: no account selected, no file, a Reel needs exactly one video, more than one file in a story, the caption is longer than 2200 characters, the time has already passed or is more than 30 days away.
