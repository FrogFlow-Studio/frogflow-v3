# Quick start: from connecting to the first paid order

keywords: start, getting started, where to begin, first steps, launch, setup, guide, tutorial, how it works, beginner, onboarding, nachat
group: start
order: 1

Five steps, after which the shop sells on its own: a person comments under a reel, gets a "Buy" button in Direct, places the order, pays and sends a receipt, and once you confirm it they get the materials. It all takes about ten minutes.

1. Connect Instagram in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#instagram-whatsapp). In detail: [How to connect Instagram](/admin/help?a=instagram-connect).
2. Set up how you get paid: countries, payment details and QR in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#payment-methods). In detail: [How to accept payment](/admin/help?a=accept-payment).
3. Check that the product has a price and a file, and that it is switched on in [Products](/admin/products).
4. Create a rule under a reel with a "Buy" button on the [Instagram](/admin/instagram) page. In detail: [The first rule with a "Buy" button](/admin/help?a=first-rule).
5. Place an order for yourself and confirm it. In detail: [The first order and receipt confirmation](/admin/help?a=first-order).

## Where things are {#where}

The menu is on the left; on a phone it is the "More" button at the bottom. Menu sections collapse: tap a group's heading and it opens. Every page heading has a "?": it opens the article about that page on the side without leaving it. The same "?" sits next to switches: it explains what exactly that switch changes.

Orders are in "Orders". Conversations with buyers are in "Inbox". Rules for comments are in "Marketing" → "Instagram". Payment, channels and notifications are in "Settings" → "Payment & settings".

## What to check after launch {#after}

- Order and receipt notifications come to you in Telegram: check the "Notifications" block in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#notify).
- The rules under the posts with the most comments have a "Buy" button. A post without that button collects comments, but brings no orders.
- Once a day look at the "Receipt review" tab in "Orders": receipts waiting for your confirmation are there.

If something went wrong, look in the "What to do if" section or write to [Support](/admin/support).
