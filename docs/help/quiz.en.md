# Product quiz

group: guide
order: 260
pages: /admin/quiz

The quiz helps a buyer choose a product: the bot asks a few questions and shows up to three matching products. Where to find it: "Menu → Marketing → Product quiz" (available when the quiz module is on).

Where the buyer sees the quiz: the "🧩 Find a product" button in the Telegram bot menu, the word "quiz" and a button in the catalog in Instagram and WhatsApp (there the buyer replies with the option number), and the "🧩 Find a product" link on the website storefront. The quiz appears once the questions are set up and saved.

## Greeting {#intro}

"Greeting before the questions" — the first message of the quiz, for example "Answer 3 questions and I'll pick a set for your age".

## Questions and options {#questions}

- Up to 5 questions: "+ Question" adds one, "Remove question" removes it.
- Each question has 2 to 6 options: "+ option" and "✕".
- "Option" — what the buyer sees on the button, up to 40 characters.
- "Tags, comma-separated" — the bot searches products by them.

Don't forget to click "Save".

## How tags work {#tags}

A tag is the beginning of a word in the product name, keywords or description. "пропис" finds "прописи", "кот" finds "кота" and "котёнок", but not "скотч". The more tags from the buyer's answers match, the higher the product ranks in the results.

Tip: if the results are empty, add keywords to your products in the product card — that makes them easier to find.

## Test the picker {#preview}

Choose one option in each question and click "Test the picker" — you'll see which products the bot will offer. "Nothing matched" means the tags didn't match any product: add tags to the answers or keywords to the products.
