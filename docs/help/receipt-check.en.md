# The receipt was not recognized, or a picture was sent instead of a receipt

keywords: receipt, chek, recognition, auto check, screenshot, receipt photo, wrong receipt, receipt check, ocr, amount does not match, fake
group: trouble
order: 50

While automatic receipt checking is not working, any picture the buyer sent at the payment step comes to you as a receipt: the order moves to "Receipt review". It may be a real receipt, a screenshot of an error or a photo of the material. You decide.

1. Open [Orders](/admin/orders), the "Receipt review" tab.
2. Open the order and look at the "Payment receipt".
3. Compare the amount, the recipient and the date with the money that came to your account.
4. The money arrived — "✅ Confirm and deliver". It did not arrive or the receipt is wrong — write to the buyer in "Inbox" or choose "Reject" in the "More" menu.

## Automatic receipt checking {#auto}

If "Receipt recognition" is connected, AI reads the receipt and checks it against the order: the notification and the order card show "matched", "suspicious" with the reasons, or "repeat", and you confirm with the button. You can turn on delivering the order right away for a matching receipt in the same block: "Deliver the order right away if the receipt matches". If recognition is not set up on the platform, the "Receipt recognition" block in [Payment and settings](/admin/settings#receipt-ocr) says so, and all receipts come to you for manual review. Even with automatic checking, compare the incoming payments from time to time: a picture can be faked.

## The buyer sent a file, not a picture {#file}

If the receipt was not saved, ask them to send a screenshot, not a file or a message forwarded from another chat.

## The buyer paid, but there is no order {#no-order}

Sometimes a person transfers money using old payment details without placing an order. Find them in "Inbox", ask what they bought, and deliver it by hand, or ask them to place the order through the bot and send the same receipt.
