# Buyer reviews

keywords: reviews, ratings, stars, rating, moderation, approve a review, hide a review, reply to a review, rating request, storefront, otzyvy, feedback

group: guide

order: 70

pages: /admin/reviews

Buyers' ratings and comments after an order is delivered. Here you decide which reviews to show on the storefront and reply to them. Where to find it: "Menu → Sales → Reviews". The section exists if the reviews module is connected. Any employee except the accountant can view it; a manager and above can decide and reply.

At the top — the average rating, how many reviews are awaiting a decision and how many have no reply.

## Filters {#filters}

- "All ratings" or "1 ★" … "5 ★".
- "With text only" — without star-only reviews.
- "No reply yet", "Awaiting approval".
- Product: "All products" or a single product.

If there are many reviews, page through them with the "Back" and "Next" buttons.

## When to ask for a rating {#ask-days}

The "Ask for a rating, days after delivery" field and "Save". The bot asks for a rating once per order, during the day by the shop's time.

- Telegram — with rating buttons.
- Instagram and WhatsApp — only if the buyer wrote within the last 24 hours; they reply with a digit from 1 to 5. If the window is closed, the request waits up to 3 days after the due date. If the order has several products, the bot asks about them one by one.
- We do not write to people who unsubscribed or blocked the bot. The request counts toward the daily limit of promotional messages to one buyer.
- No more than 50 requests go out per run. An order older than the set term plus 33 days is not asked about.

## Show on the storefront or hide {#moderation}

Each review has a status: "Awaiting decision: not shown on the storefront", "Shown on the storefront", "Hidden from the storefront". A new review is not visible on the storefront until you press "Show on storefront". "Hide" removes it.

## Older reviews {#legacy}

Reviews left before approval existed are not visible on the storefront and are marked "Older review". "Approve all older reviews" shows them all at once; new reviews and ones you hid do not change. If there are many older ones, press again — the panel tells you how many are left.

## Reply to a review {#reply}

1. Press "Reply" and write the text in "Your reply to the review".
2. Tick where to send it (at least one).
3. Press "Send reply".

"Write with AI" writes a draft into the field; you send it yourself, after editing.

## Send to the buyer as a private message {#reply-buyer}

With the tick on, the reply goes to the buyer in their channel on behalf of the shop. If the buyer cannot be reached (blocked the bot, the Instagram window is closed), the panel says "Reply not delivered".

## Show on the storefront under the review {#reply-store}

With the tick on, the reply is visible on the storefront under the review once the review itself is approved. "Remove from the storefront" takes down the public reply; a private message already sent to the buyer is not recalled.
