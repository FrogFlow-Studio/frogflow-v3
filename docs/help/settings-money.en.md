The "Money" topic on the [Payment and settings](/admin/settings) page: the payment details buyers pay you by, and links to paying for your subscription and AI balance. Everything in this topic is seen and changed by the shop owner only.

## Payment methods by country {#payment-methods}

The buyer chooses a country, sees your payment details and QR code, transfers the money and sends a receipt. Until there is at least one country, buyers cannot place an order.

1. Press "+ Country".
2. "Country code" is two letters, for example KZ; "Name" is what the buyer will see, for example "Kazakhstan"; "Currency" is three letters, for example KZT.
3. "Payment details and instructions" is the text the buyer sees after checkout: card or account number, recipient, what to write in the comment.
4. "QR code (image)": upload a payment QR if you like.
5. Leave the "Active" checkbox ticked and press "Save".

"Order" sets the position in the list. The first country is the home one: the price is calculated by it while the buyer's country is unknown. Unticking "Active" hides the country from buyers without deleting it (the list shows an "off" mark). "Edit" and "Delete" are in the country row.

## Prices in all payment currencies {#all-prices}

A switch under the country list. On: the product card in Telegram, Instagram and WhatsApp shows the price in every payment currency, for example "1500 ₸ / 490 ₽", and when paying for a digital order the buyer chooses a country and sees the amount in its currency. Off: one price.

Works with the "Multi-currency" module and two or three different currencies in the payment methods; otherwise one price is shown. Saved immediately.

## Payment history {#billing}

Payments and invoices for your FrogFlow subscription are on a separate page, [Payment history](/admin/billing). Seen by the owner (and the accountant).

## AI balance {#ai-balance}

How much money is left for AI features (consultant, text assistant, smart search, receipt recognition) and how to top it up is on the [AI balance](/admin/ai-balance) page.
