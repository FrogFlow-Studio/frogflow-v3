The "Team" topic on the [Payment and settings](/admin/settings) page: who else works in the shop's admin panel, and inviting other authors to the platform.

## Team {#team}

Available with the "Multiple admins with roles" module; seen and changed by the owner only. Each staff member signs in with their own login, and all their actions are signed with this login in the order history and in the [activity log](/admin/log).

Roles in the list:

- "Administrator — everything except the plan, payments, the team and deleting the shop": settings, bot texts, activity log, orders, products.
- "Manager — orders, products, chat, broadcasts".
- "Viewer — view and export only": any change is rejected.
- "Accountant — orders, payments, receipts, revenue and order export without customer contacts": changes nothing.

## Add a user {#team-add}

1. Press "+ User".
2. "Username": Latin letters, digits, "_", at least 3 characters; it is unique across the whole platform.
3. "Name": for yourself, optional.
4. Choose the "Role" and set a "Password" of at least 8 characters.
5. Press "Save" and give the person the username and password.

"Edit" next to a staff member changes the name, role and password ("New password (leave empty to keep)"); the username cannot be changed. "Delete": the person can no longer sign in. "Journal" opens their actions.

## Invite by link {#team-invite}

The person comes up with a username and password themselves using your link.

1. Press "Invite by link".
2. Enter the "Name" (optional) and the "Role".
3. Press "Create link" and "Copy", and send the link to the person.

The link is shown once, works once and is valid for 7 days. Until someone registers with it, the invitation is visible under "Waiting for a reply"; "Revoke" makes the link invalid.

## Invite an author {#invite}

Seen by the owner and administrators. A link for another author who would benefit from FrogFlow: they get a discount on the setup fee in their first invoice, and you get a month of subscription credited, but only after they actually pay for a plan. The credit is granted manually by the platform operator, who checks every invitation: no credit is given for registering yourself.

"Copy link" copies it. Below are counters: how many registered with the link, how many paid for a plan and for how many you have already been credited a month. The names of invited people are not shown.
