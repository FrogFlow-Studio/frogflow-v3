# Share your shop: links, QR codes and tags

This page collects links to your storefront and Telegram bot, QR codes and ready texts, so buyers find you where you talk to them. Where to find it: "Menu → Marketing → Share". The page is available to shops that sell and is open to any staff member.

If there is neither an open storefront nor a Telegram bot, there is nothing to share yet — the page will offer "Open settings".

## Your links {#links}

Links to the "Storefront" and the "Telegram bot". "Copy" puts the link on the clipboard. Under each link there is a QR code — you can print it on a business card, price tag or packaging.

## Ready texts {#texts}

Texts for Instagram (for example, for the profile header or a post) and for WhatsApp, with the link already in them. "Text language" — which of the four languages to show the text in. Edit the text as you like before pasting it.

The link in these texts carries a channel tag: visits and orders from it appear in [Analytics](/admin/analytics), in the sources table.

## Separate links per channel {#channels}

A link of its own to the storefront and the bot for Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram and TikTok. Put each channel's link in that channel — then [Analytics](/admin/analytics) shows where buyers came from and how much they ordered.

## Your own tag {#custom}

"Your own tag (for example, “blogger Anna”)" — think of a short name, and the page instantly makes links to the storefront and the bot with that tag. Example: give a blogger a link tagged blogger_anna, then check in "Analytics" how many orders she brought. If the tag doesn't fit (for example, it is too long), a hint appears under the field.
