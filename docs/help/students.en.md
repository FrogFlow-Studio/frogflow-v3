# Online school students

keywords: students, learners, course, progress, falling behind, access, quizzes, homework, students export, school, ucheniki

group: guide

order: 150

pages: /admin/students

Everyone who bought courses, and how they are going through the lessons. Where to find it: "Menu → Learning → Students". The section exists if learning is connected.

## Filters and export {#filters}

- "All courses" or a single course.
- The "Only those behind" tick leaves students who are behind the course schedule.
- "Download CSV" — the list of students of the chosen course (for a manager and above).

## What you see for a student {#progress}

- "… of … done, … open" — how many lessons are done and how many are already open by the schedule; "not opened yet" — they have not entered the course.
- "behind", "access ended".
- "accepted …, waiting …, needs rework …" — homework.
- "quizzes …" — passed quizzes and the number of attempts.

The page only shows data; work is reviewed in [Homework](/admin/homework).
