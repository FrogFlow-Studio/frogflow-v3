# Support chat

keywords: support, help, write to support, contact, question, problem, error, screenshot, request, tech support
group: guide
order: 540
pages: /admin/support

Here you write to us, and we reply in the same chat. The conversation is saved, and we see it next to your shop. Where to find it: "Menu → Settings → Support"; the number next to the item is new replies. We usually reply within a few hours of working time: Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 19:00 Almaty time.

If the section is not on yet, the page says so: then write to us in Telegram or WhatsApp as usual.

## Write {#write}

1. Describe the question in the "Describe your question or problem" field. Help articles may appear under the field with the heading "This may help:": take a look, the answer may already be there.
2. If needed, press "Attach a screenshot" (a JPEG or PNG image up to 5 MB). "Remove" removes the picture.
3. Press "Send".

If you came to support with a button from another page, the field shows which page you are writing from: we will see that too.

The owner, administrators and managers can write. With the "Viewer" role the chat is read-only.

## Status and new replies {#status}

Above the chat is the request status:

- "sent": we received the message;
- "in progress": we are looking into it;
- "waiting for your reply": we need details from you;
- "resolved": the question is closed; if it comes back, just write again.

New replies load by themselves every 30 seconds; "Refresh" checks right away. When you open the chat, replies are marked as read.
