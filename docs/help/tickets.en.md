# Requests: buyer questions

keywords: requests, tickets, customer support, contact, complaint, question, helpdesk, rating, overdue, assignee, obrashcheniya, tikety

group: guide

order: 50

pages: /admin/tickets

Questions buyers asked with the "Contact" button in the bot. You reply in "Inbox" or right from here, and here you keep the status, the assignee and team notes. Where to find it: "Menu → Sales → Requests", the number next to it is open ones. The section exists if the requests and manager chat modules are connected.

Any employee except the accountant can view it; a manager and above can change things.

## Filters and ratings {#filters}

"Open", "Waiting for customer", "Closed", "All". Above the list — the buyers' average rating and how many low ratings (1–2) there are.

## Remind about an unanswered request {#overdue}

The "Remind me about unanswered requests:" list — "don’t remind" or "after … h". If the buyer waits for a reply longer than the chosen time, the request is marked "overdue", and you get one reminder in Telegram.

## Request status {#status}

The "Open", "Waiting for customer", "Closed" buttons.

- "Waiting for customer" — you replied and are waiting for their reaction.
- "Closed" — the buyer gets a "Request closed" message and a request to rate the reply from 1 to 5: in Telegram with star buttons, in Instagram and WhatsApp by replying with a digit. If the buyer has already rated, we do not ask again. The rating appears in "Customer rating".

## Assignee {#assignee}

The "Assignee" list — "Unassigned" or an employee of the shop's team. If there is no assignee, it becomes whoever replies first from this page.

## Replying to a buyer {#reply}

The "Write a reply — it goes to the customer in their channel and appears in the Chat" field and the "Send reply" button. The reply goes the same way as from [Inbox](/admin/chat), and in the same way pauses the AI consultant in this dialog. "Saved replies" insert a ready phrase; they are set up in "Inbox".

"Open conversation" opens the whole conversation with the buyer.

## Notes and history {#note}

The "Note for the team — the customer does not see it" field and the "Add note" button. "History" shows who changed the status and the assignee and wrote notes, and when.
