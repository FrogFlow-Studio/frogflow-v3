# The order was placed for the wrong product

keywords: wrong product, mistaken order, different material, order for the wrong thing, mixed up product, someone else's product, wrong material, ne tot tovar
group: trouble
order: 90

Most often this happens when the buyer commented under a different post, and the rule has a "Buy" button with another post's product, or when a rule "for all posts" sells a single product.

1. Do not press "Confirm and deliver" until you have checked: otherwise the buyer gets the wrong material.
2. Ask the buyer in [Inbox](/admin/chat) what they wanted to buy.
3. If the receipt has already arrived and the amount matches, reject the mistaken order and ask them to order the right product, or deliver the right material by hand.
4. Check the post's rule on the [Instagram](/admin/instagram) page: the product in the "Buy" button must match the post.

## How to prevent it {#prevent}

- Put a separate rule with its own product on every post that sells.
- Use the "All account posts" rule only with keywords that clearly name the product.
- Pay attention to the warning "The product … is not mentioned in the post" when saving a rule.

An order is created only on the buyer's explicit consent: "+", "yes", "да", "хочу" or a tap on "Buy". A heart or a question in reply to the message does not count as an order.
